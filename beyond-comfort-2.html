About us

Growth outside the comfort zone

Beyond Comfort is a school-based and juvenile detention centre-based mental resilience program supporting troubled, at-risk, and introverted young people to build confidence, emotional strength, and positive life skills.

The program helps participants step outside their comfort zones, strengthen self-belief, and develop healthy coping strategies in a safe and supportive environment, while encouraging social connection and personal growth.

We have supported over 100 young people across Years 7–12. Programs run throughout the school term or as tailored sessions and are delivered in small groups of up to 10 participants to ensure meaningful engagement and personalised support.

Who it's for

Young people in Years 7–12 across school and juvenile detention centre settings, delivered in small groups of up to 10 for meaningful, personalised engagement.
