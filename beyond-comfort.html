Growth outside the comfort zone

Beyond Comfort delivers mental resilience and wellbeing programs across primary schools, high schools, and juvenile detention centres — supporting young people to build confidence, emotional strength, and positive life skills.

We also work with workplaces, running corporate sessions and coaching team leaders on how to have genuine wellbeing conversations with the people they manage.

We have supported over 100 young people across primary and high school years. Programs run throughout the school term or as tailored sessions and are delivered in small groups of up to 10 participants to ensure meaningful engagement and personalised support.

Who it's for

Primary and high school students, young people in juvenile detention centres, and workplace teams. Delivered in small groups of up to 10 for meaningful, personalised engagement.
